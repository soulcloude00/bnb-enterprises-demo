MANA Skanda — The Right Life

Sarjapur–Varthur Road, Bangalore — developed by Mana Projects & Skanda Avani. B&B Enterprises: construction contractor.

Images: developer’s official project site

The project

2, 3, 3.5 & 4 BHK homes in an integrated township

A 100+ acre child-centric integrated township on Sarjapur–Varthur Road, planned as five provinces — Solitaire, Lake, Sports, Forest and Beach — around a reclaimed lake, a forest and a sports arena, with 84% open space.
