The project

2, 3 & 4 BHK apartments

A nature-forward community of 2, 3 and 4 BHK residences on the Sarjapur–Whitefield Main Road in Varthur, opposite Brigade Utopia — with an urban forest, eco pond and a project-level sky walk.
