Core service

Mivan construction

Aluminium-formwork construction for residential buildings — where walls and slabs are cast together, floor after floor, in a precise repeating cycle.

  • Monolithic casting of walls and slabs for a strong, seamless structure
  • A fast, repeatable floor cycle suited to high-rise residential towers
  • Accurate, smooth concrete surfaces that reduce plastering and rework
  • Consistent quality across every floor and every tower

Core service

Block construction

Block masonry built by skilled, supervised crews — the dependable, flexible method for villas and low- to mid-rise residences.

  • Well-suited to independent villas and varied architectural layouts
  • Experienced masonry crews with on-site supervision
  • Flexible sequencing alongside structural and services work
  • Built to the developer’s specification and finish standard
