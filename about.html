Our story

Built on experience

B&B Enterprises was founded by Prakash Burman, who has led the company for 20+ years in construction.

What began as a family business has grown into a contractor trusted by Bangalore’s leading developers — with 50+ completed projects, including high-rise towers, and a 5,000+ workforce.
